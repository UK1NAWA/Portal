Privacy Policy

Baguio College of Technology (BCT) — Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Baguio College of Technology ("BCT", "we", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of all students, teachers, and administrators who use this Student Portal System. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect and store the following personal information:

  • Full name, email address, and contact number
  • Username and encrypted password
  • Learner Reference Number (LRN) assigned by DepEd
  • Year level, strand, and section assignment
  • Academic records including grades and quarterly assessments
  • Attendance records (present, absent, late)
  • Tasks, announcements, and schedule data related to your section
  • Login activity and system usage logs

3. How We Use Your Data

Your personal data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating your identity when you log in
  • Displaying your academic records, schedule, and attendance
  • Allowing teachers to record and submit grades and attendance
  • Sending announcements and tasks from teachers to students
  • Administering user accounts and section assignments
  • Maintaining system security and audit trails

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal data to any third party outside of BCT without your explicit consent, except as required by law.

4. Who Can Access Your Data

  • Students — can only view their own grades, attendance, schedule, and profile
  • Teachers — can view data of students assigned to their sections only
  • Administrators — can access all user accounts for management purposes, bound by confidentiality obligations

5. Data Security

We implement the following measures to protect your data:

  • Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt hashing — they are never stored in plain text
  • Sessions expire automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity
  • All database queries use prepared statements to prevent unauthorized access
  • System activity is recorded in audit logs accessible only to administrators
  • Access to the system is role-restricted — each user only sees what is relevant to their role

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for the duration of your enrollment at BCT and for a reasonable period after graduation or withdrawal, as required by DepEd regulations. Accounts that are rejected during registration are removed after 90 days.

7. Your Rights Under RA 10173

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Be informed of how your data is collected and used
  • Access your personal data stored in this system
  • Request correction of inaccurate information in your profile
  • Object to the processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) if you believe your rights have been violated

8. Cookies and Sessions

This system uses session cookies to keep you logged in during your visit. These cookies are temporary, expire when you close your browser or after 30 minutes of inactivity, and do not track you across other websites.

9. Changes to This Policy

BCT reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy as needed. Any significant changes will be communicated through the portal's announcement system.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related concerns, please contact the school administration at BCT. You may also reach the National Privacy Commission at complaints@privacy.gov.ph.
